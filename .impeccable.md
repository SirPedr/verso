# Verso — Design Context & Brief

## Design Context

### Users
Three readers Verso writes for:
- **Spotify referrals** — followed the link from the artist profile, here to read the lyrics behind the songs.
- **Returning readers** — bookmarked Verso, come back for new posts. They already know the voice.
- **First-timers** — landed via a share/tweet, no context. Need a quick read on what this is before they invest.

All read in a private, low-light context (late at night, on a phone, during a quiet moment). The content is melancholic and introspective; readers are not "browsing" — they're *settling in*.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: voiced, melancholic, printerly.

The 404 page sets the tone: "Perdido nos versos." Wordplay, intimate, a little sad, gently literary. The rest of the site should sound like that line wrote it.

Anti-references: SaaS-onboarding cheer, Medium polish, generic "literary blog" templates with Newsreader-on-dark.

### Aesthetic Direction
Old-print sensibility rendered on a screen at night. Dark theme stays — it matches both the content (melancholic) and the reading context (late, alone). But the dark surface should feel like a printed page held under a single lamp, not a glowing dashboard.

- Single-column layout with hanging punctuation, large title, drop cap on first paragraph.
- Generous negative space; restraint over density.
- Asymmetry where it earns it (title hanging large, ragged-right body, italic subtitles letting paragraphs breathe).
- The I-beam mark in the header is the project's visual signature — treat it as a typographer's character that can recur (between posts, as a separator, replacing the © glyph in the footer).

### Design Principles
1. **Voice everywhere, or nowhere.** The 404 line is the standard, not the exception. Every framing copy moment should sound written, not generated.
2. **Dark as paper, not as glow.** No accent glow, no gradients, no "tech" cyan-on-black. Light text on dark = ink on coated paper at night.
3. **Distinctive type or no type.** Generic web fonts (Inter, Newsreader, etc.) are forbidden. The serif is the most visible single design decision.
4. **The reader is settled in, not skimming.** Optimize for slow reading: long line-heights, ample top/bottom margin around posts, no infinite-feed energy.
5. **One mark, recurring.** The I-beam is a glyph, not a logo. Use it as a print mark (between sections, as a footer ornament, in a 404, etc.).

## Design Brief — Site Redesign

### Feature Summary
Redesign of all four templates (`index`, `posts/[id]`, `about`, `404`) to (a) fix layout and accessibility bugs, (b) replace AI-slop typography with a distinctive serif/sans pair, and (c) extend the "Perdido nos versos" voice across the site. Scope is the whole site, not a single feature.

### Primary User Action
**Read a post, comfortably, late at night.** Everything else (navigation, framing, About) is in service of this one act.

### Design Direction
Old-print, voiced, restrained. Per Design Principles above. Dark theme retained; tokens already use `oklch`-friendly hex but should be migrated to `oklch()` proper, with neutrals subtly tinted toward the accent hue (currently `#7f98ca` — a desaturated dusk-blue).

### Layout Strategy

**Home (`index.astro`)**
- Quiet epigraph at top of the post list, pulled in spirit from the About copy: a single line on solitude / angústia / busca por significado. Treated as small italic serif, not as a hero.
- Post list flows below. No filter UI (`filterByQueryTrigger` to be removed). No grouping for "Música #" series — the prefix in the title is the only signal, intentionally.
- Vary spacing between list items modestly (not uniform 20px gaps); long titles get more breathing room than short ones.
- Title clamping (`text-overflow: ellipsis; white-space: nowrap`) must be removed — long titles wrap.

**Post (`posts/[id].astro`)**
- **Title hangs large** at top. Big serif, left-aligned (not centered).
- **Italic subtitle** below title, smaller, in tertiary color.
- **Date** rendered as `<time>` between subtitle and body, small, italic, also tertiary.
- **Drop cap** on the first letter of the first paragraph. Old-print convention: 3-line height, optical alignment with body baseline.
- **Hanging punctuation** via `hanging-punctuation: first allow-end last;`.
- **Body**: ragged-right (`text-align: left`), `text-wrap: pretty`, `hyphens: auto` with `lang="pt-BR"` correctly set on `<html>`.
- Lyric posts (the `Música #` ones) keep their existing left-aligned, narrow-column style — the drop cap may not apply. Small visual differentiation between prose and verse.
- Footer of post: a centered I-beam glyph (small, tertiary color) as the end-of-text mark, before the global footer.

**About (`about.astro`)**
- Same hanging-title treatment.
- "Spotify logo abaixo" copy → either deliver an inline Spotify glyph or rewrite the line. Rewrite preferred for now (no extra dependency): "Meu catálogo está no Spotify." with the link styled as a single accent word.
- Generous spacing.

**404 (`404.astro`)**
- Keep the existing copy ("Perdido nos versos" / "Parece que a página que você procura se perdeu...") — it's the voice anchor for the whole site.
- Add a quiet "Voltar ao início" link below the description, italic serif, accent color.
- Optionally: replace the giant "404" numerals with the I-beam glyph rendered very large, with "Perdido nos versos" as the H1. (Open question — to revisit during implementation.)

**Header / Footer**
- Header: wrap I-beam SVG + "Verso" wordmark in `<a href="/">`. Active page indicator in nav (current page = primary text color, others = secondary).
- Footer: **non-fixed**, sits at end of document flow. Uses the I-beam as the © replacement: `⌶ MMXXVI · Verso` (Roman numerals or current-year — implementer's call). Small, italic, tertiary. No `rgba` background — just transparent on the page.

### Key States

| Page | State | Treatment |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Home | Default | Epigraph + post list (13 entries today) |
| Home | Empty (no posts) | Single italic line: "Em silêncio." (or similar — not "No posts found") |
| Post | Default | Hanging title, drop cap, ragged body |
| Post | Long-form prose | Drop cap, hanging punctuation |
| Post | Short verse / lyrics | Left-aligned narrow column, no drop cap, no justification |
| About | Default | Hanging title, two paragraphs, Spotify link |
| 404 | Default | "Perdido nos versos" + "Voltar ao início" |

### Interaction Model
Read-only site. Interactions are minimal and should feel like turning a page, not clicking a button.
- Clickable surfaces: header logo (→ home), nav links, post-list items, About-page Spotify link, 404 home link.
- Hover: links underline (already in [base-layout.astro:60](src/layouts/base-layout.astro#L60)). Keep.
- Visited links: must be styled (currently relies on browser default purple, which clashes with the dark palette). Use `--content-color-tertiary` for visited links in body content.
- Focus states: visible focus ring on all interactive elements (currently none defined).
- No motion required. No animations. The site should feel still.

### Content Requirements
- **Home epigraph**: one line, ~6–10 words, italic. Sourced from About text in spirit (solidão / angústia / busca por significado). Implementer to draft 2–3 options for sign-off during `/clarify`.
- **Footer**: rewrite from "© 2026 Verso. Todos os direitos reservados." to something with voice. Draft: `⌶ MMXXVI · Verso · escrito em silêncio` (or similar — implementer to propose).
- **404 home link**: `Voltar ao início` (italic, accent color).
- **About Spotify line**: replace "logo abaixo" — draft: `Meu catálogo está no Spotify.`
- **`<title>`** convention: `[Page] · Verso`. Home: `Verso`. About: `Sobre · Verso`. 404: `Perdido nos versos · Verso`. Posts: `[post title] · Verso`.
- **`lang="pt-BR"`** on `<html>` (currently `en` — bug).

### Typography Direction
- **Body sans**: replace Inter. Look outside the impeccable reflex-reject list. Candidates to evaluate: a humanist sans with character (e.g., Söhne-style alternatives, or something from Pangram/Klim/Velvetyne/Future Fonts). Implementer to propose 2 candidates with samples during `/typeset`.
- **Display serif**: replace Newsreader. Old-style or transitional with personality. Implementer to propose 2 candidates.
- **Type scale**: 5-step modular scale, ratio ≥1.25. Body 1.0625rem, line-height ~1.7 for prose on dark. Headings via `clamp()` for fluid sizing on long-form pages.
- **Lyric posts** stay in the body serif (not display) — they're verse, not headlines.

### Open Questions (to resolve during implementation)
- Should the 404 page replace the numerals "404" with the I-beam glyph, or keep the numerals? Try both, pick the one that feels more on-brand.
- For the home epigraph: drafted line vs. quoted line from About (e.g., "Solidão, angústia e busca por significado.") — pick during `/clarify`.
- Drop cap rendering on PT-BR words with diacritics (`Á`, `É`, `Ô`, etc.): test and ensure the cap aligns optically.
- Visited-link color: tertiary works for body content, but on the home post-list we may want visited posts to dim their date too. Decide during `/polish`.
